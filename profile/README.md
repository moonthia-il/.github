<h1 align="center">Moonthia</h1>

<p align="center">An independent software studio in Israel. We build custom software for businesses and tools for online communities.</p>

## What we do

Moonthia is a small, independent studio, founded in 2026. We design, build and run software ourselves, from the first idea to the day it goes live and beyond.

## For businesses

Every business has work that is slow, repetitive or spread across too many tools. We build programs made around the way your business actually works, instead of asking you to change to fit a ready-made product. That can be:

- **Customer service tools.** One place where a team answers customers by chat and by phone, sees who is waiting, and knows how the day is going.
- **Tools that take over repetitive work**, so your people can spend their time on things that need a person.
- **Links between the systems you already use**, so information only has to be entered once.

## For online communities

Running a community is a lot of work: welcoming new people, keeping things friendly, and keeping up with everything that happens. We build tools that take care of the routine parts, so the people running a community can spend their time on the people in it.

## How we work

- **We listen first.** We start by understanding the problem, then build what solves it.
- **Plain words.** We explain what we are building in a way anyone can follow, with no jargon.
